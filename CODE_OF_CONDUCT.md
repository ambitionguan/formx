# Code of Conduct

FormX follows a simple rule: be respectful and keep technical discussion focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus disagreements on code, design, and maintainability.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of project discussions.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior harms the project community.
